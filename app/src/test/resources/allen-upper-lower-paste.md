# Allen’s Upper / Lower paste (14 Sep)

Create a routine → paste. These four sessions must import: names, sets, rep ranges, per-leg / per-side, and timed holds.

## Upper A (strength)

1. Barbell bench press — 4×4–6
2. Weighted pull-up or lat pulldown — 4×4–6
3. Overhead press — 3×5–8
4. Chest-supported row — 3×6–8
5. Lateral raises — 3×10–15
6. Abs: hanging knee raise — 3×8–12
7. Static: dead hang — 2×20–40s (ligament/grip)

## Lower A (strength)

1. Back squat — 4×4–6
2. Romanian deadlift — 3×5–8
3. Walking lunges — 3×8/leg
4. Leg curl — 3×8–12
5. Standing calf raise — 3×8–12
6. Abs: cable crunch or weighted plank — 3×10–15 / 3×30–45s
7. Static: wall sit — 2×30–45s

## Upper B (muscle)

1. Incline DB press — 3×8–12
2. Seated cable row — 3×8–12
3. DB shoulder press — 3×8–12
4. Lat pulldown (neutral or wide) — 3×10–12
5. Face pulls — 3×12–15
6. Arms: curl + triceps pushdown — 2–3×10–15 each
7. Abs: ab wheel or dead bug — 3×8–12
8. Static: scapular hang or Y-hold — 2×20–30s

## Lower B (muscle)

1. Front squat or goblet squat — 3×8–12
2. Hip thrust or glute bridge — 3×8–12
3. Bulgarian split squat — 3×8–10/leg
4. Leg press or hack squat — 3×10–15
5. Seated calf raise — 3×10–15
6. Abs: side plank — 2–3×20–40s/side
7. Static: deep squat hold (assisted ok) — 2×30–45s
