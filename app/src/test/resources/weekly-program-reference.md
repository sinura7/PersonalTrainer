# Allen weekly program (2026-09-13)

Owner program. Paste-importer, catalog gaps, timed vs reps, Extra/cardio.

## Week
Mon — Upper A (strength bias)
Tue — Lower A (strength bias)
Wed — optional easy cardio + mobility, or rest
Thu — Upper B (hypertrophy bias)
Fri — Lower B (hypertrophy bias)
Sat — cardio + flexibility block
Sun — rest
Warm up every lift day: 5–8 min light cardio, then 2–3 ramp sets on the first compound.

## Upper A (strength)
Barbell bench press — 4×4–6
Weighted pull-up or lat pulldown — 4×4–6
Overhead press — 3×5–8
Chest-supported row — 3×6–8
Lateral raises — 3×10–15
Abs: hanging knee raise — 3×8–12
Static: dead hang — 2×20–40s (ligament/grip)

## Lower A (strength)
Back squat — 4×4–6
Romanian deadlift — 3×5–8
Walking lunges — 3×8/leg
Leg curl — 3×8–12
Standing calf raise — 3×8–12
Abs: cable crunch or weighted plank — 3×10–15 / 3×30–45s
Static: wall sit — 2×30–45s

## Upper B (muscle)
Incline DB press — 3×8–12
Seated cable row — 3×8–12
DB shoulder press — 3×8–12
Lat pulldown (neutral or wide) — 3×10–12
Face pulls — 3×12–15
Arms: curl + triceps pushdown — 2–3×10–15 each
Abs: ab wheel or dead bug — 3×8–12
Static: scapular hang or Y-hold — 2×20–30s

## Lower B (muscle)
Front squat or goblet squat — 3×8–12
Hip thrust or glute bridge — 3×8–12
Bulgarian split squat — 3×8–10/leg
Leg press or hack squat — 3×10–15
Seated calf raise — 3×10–15
Abs: side plank — 2–3×20–40s/side
Static: deep squat hold (assisted ok) — 2×30–45s

## Cardio (2×/week)
Wed (optional): 20–30 min easy Zone 2 (walk, bike, row)
Sat: 25–40 min Zone 2, or 8–12 min intervals if you feel good (e.g. 30s hard / 90s easy)

## Flexibility / ligament block (Sat, ~15–20 min) — hold each stretch 45–60s
Hip flexor stretch, couch stretch, or 90/90 hips
Hamstring stretch
Thoracic openers / doorway chest stretch
Ankle rocks + calf stretch
Finish with 2–3 slow controlled articular rotations (shoulders, hips, spine)

## Progression
Add weight when you hit the top of the rep range on all work sets with clean form.
Rest 2–3 min on heavy compounds, 60–90s on accessories.
Deload every 6th week (cut volume ~40%, keep light intensity).
