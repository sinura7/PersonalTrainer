### UX11 / UX23 — Plan workout write recovery
- Source-backed problem: the day picker closed before asynchronous save finished, while each tap launched another write. Failure after routine/pin/rule insertion left no safe way to finish publication.
- Chosen refinement: admit one action synchronously; retain the picker and show Updating plan; remember completed workout substeps and offer one reachable Retry. Freeze the acceptance instant and chosen hour; finish only the accepted rule/day after midnight or a date-changing zone shift, preserving existing rows and the ordinary anti-backfill policy. Success closes the picker or opens the exact accepted routine editor. Removal and ordering retain their existing idempotent operations.
- Alternative and tradeoff: a durable action receipt would extend process-death recovery but requires a separately specified persistence packet. The current pending operation stays in the retained ViewModel; saved Room rows survive independently. Back remains available after failure. Cardio/auxiliary helpers receive busy feedback and honest inspection copy, without an unsafe automatic Retry.
- Product rules: Instrument's one primary action, weekly Plan additions, Home-owned Start and captured-history ownership remain intact (ADR-005, ADR-015, ADR-021, ADR-026). No schema, backup, signing or version change.
- Acceptance: `PlanDayWriteRecoveryTest` covers duplicate/slow writes, insertion/publication faults, exact IDs/hour, future-week visibility, midnight/week/zone rollover, preserved terminal rows, no unrelated backfill or overdue reminders, removal and reorder rollback. `PlanDayWriteRecoveryRenderTest` covers saving, failure, Retry, completion and retained-VM saved-state restoration across 12 profiles. Final-source gate, native, review and integration evidence belongs to [the packet report](https://github.com/sinura7/PersonalTrainer/pull/477); physical-phone acceptance and broader UX23/UX24 remain open.

### UX09 — Library selection into a first routine
- Source-backed problem: Create routine dismissed Library's chosen exercise and opened an empty editor, requiring another search.
- Chosen refinement: carry the canonical exercise ID in the new-editor route and its SavedStateHandle. Prepare the selected lift with the ordinary defaults before presenting the editor. Retry reuses the same routine and checks the stored membership; a consumed intent cannot undo a deliberate removal after restoration. Existing-routine routes ignore this intent.
- Alternative and tradeoff: staging the lift until Save would introduce a second cancellation model. Retain the established write-through contract instead: dismissing the selection sheet creates nothing; editor Back keeps written lifts and removes a new empty stub. Back waits for an accepted preparation write. Library retains its existing back-stack entry, query, filters and scroll state.
- Recovery and rules: a failed preparation offers reachable Retry; an unavailable lift offers Back to Library without substituting another exercise. Recovery content scrolls with large text. Instrument, normal routine defaults, offline ownership, schema and backup formats remain unchanged (ADR-005, ADR-026). Restoration uses the existing draft contract, not a durable transaction receipt across arbitrary process termination.
- Acceptance: the editor's 70-case ViewModel suite and one small-screen recovery journey passed together (71 executed checks). `RoutineEditorInitialExerciseRenderTest` defines the required size/font matrix plus RTL/reduced motion; `LibraryRoutineHandoffInstrumentedTest` uses actual AppNav for dismissal, Create, Activity recreation, Save and return to the same search. Final gate, reviews, connected execution and integration are reported by the packet; phone acceptance remains unobserved.
