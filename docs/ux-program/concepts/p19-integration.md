# Field purpose

Not the whole-app integration. Reviews blocked calling this P19. A physical screen reader stays with the phone check.

## What a field says

Search carries "Search exercises" as its state, and the routine title carries "Routine name" the same way, empty and after text is typed. The words in the box stay the words that are read. A label pasted over the box would hide "Back squat" and "Upper A".

## What this packet does not close

Walking the whole app with TalkBack, large text on every screen, and reduced-motion timing on a phone are still the owner's check. This packet does not claim those.
