# P17 settings units

Labeled before the behavior is treated as done. The phone check stays later.

## Weight and distance

Weight is pounds or kilograms. Distance is kilometres or miles. The four pairings are all allowed.

Until a distance is chosen, miles follow pounds and kilometres follow kilograms. That is the reading for an old backup that has no distance choice. Choosing kilometres or miles stores that choice. Changing the weight unit afterwards does not change it.

## Stored numbers

A stored weight stays kilograms. A stored distance stays kilometres. Changing the labels changes how those numbers are shown. It does not rewrite them.

A distance typed into a live session or the activity composer keeps the unit on the label at the time it was typed. Changing the preference before that entry is saved does not read the same digits as the other unit.

## Where to find them

Settings opens on a list. Display, Rest timer, and Backup are rows on that list. Display holds weight, distance, and hours. Backup is where export and restore live.

## Coming back

The open settings page is remembered on the settings screen. A display choice that could not be saved stays on screen, including after leaving that page, until a later save of a display choice succeeds.

## Debug and the everyday app

Temper Debug can show an update row. The everyday app does not. Those two screens are checked as two screens.
