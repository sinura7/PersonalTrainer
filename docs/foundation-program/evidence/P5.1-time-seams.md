## Behavior contracts

**Domain is platform-neutral.** `domain/` does not import `java.time`,
`java.text.NumberFormat`, `java.util.Locale`, Android, Room, or
Compose. `tools/check-domain-seams.py` ratchets that.

**Time is a four-tuple.** `CapturedCivilTime` stores UTC millis, IANA
zone id, UTC offset seconds, and local epoch day. Display uses the
captured local date.

**DST is explicit.** America/New_York 2026-03-08 02:30 either shifts
forward to 03:30 EDT or is rejected. 2026-11-01 01:30 records either
UTC−4 or UTC−5. Rest timers stay `elapsedRealtime`.

**Quantity math is locale-free.** `WeightConverter.volumeDisplayWhole`
is the single whole-number volume. Grouped printing is
`QuantityFormat`.

**IDs are injected.** `IdPort` is the domain seam. `IdFactory` is the
JVM adapter.
