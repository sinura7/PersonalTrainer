## Audit finding closed on this page

Home's last-session tiles used `TrainingInsights.history`, the 30-day
heat graph. A finished workout older than that window left Home
showing "—" for last session and days since, while History still knew
the session. Home now reads `summaries.latest()`. Days since uses the
captured `localEpochDay`. Volume comes from the summary; a zero-volume
day falls back to working sets or cardio minutes instead of inventing
kilograms.

Reduced motion is re-read in `MainActivity.onResume`, so a Settings
change applies without process death.
