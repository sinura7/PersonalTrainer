## Behavior contracts

**One envelope.** `ActivitySession` holds strength-only, cardio-only,
and mixed typed blocks. Cardio is never a fake catalog exercise.

**Cardio-only days need zero strength rows.** A cardio session stores
no `StrengthSet` and no legacy `SetLog`.

**Two completed activities can share one local date.** Optional
`occurrenceId` distinguishes them.

**Records carry origin, source, and captured time.** `LIVE` /
`BACKDATED` / `IMPORTED` plus a `CapturedCivilTime` four-tuple.

**One live at a time.** A second live start is rejected.
`ActivityRules.confirm` is the only write; discarded drafts write
nothing. Future local dates are rejected. Templates do not rewrite
history.
